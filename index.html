01 / OUR BEGINNINGS
A PLACE TO PAUSE

Rooted in ritual.
Made for everyone.

COFFEY began with a simple thought: life moves fast, but the good stuff shouldn't. So we opened our doors to serve coffee worth slowing down for, food that brings people together, and a little breathing room in the middle of it all.

Today, we work with growers who care for their land, cook with what's in season, and save you a seat. That's our kind of progress.

GOOD QUESTIONS, GOOD ANSWERS

A little help.

Where can I find you? +

You'll find us at 18 Willow Lane, Portland, OR. We're open daily from 7:30 am to 9 pm. Pop in—we'll put the kettle on.

Can I book a table? +

Of course. Use the booking form below for groups of up to eight. For larger gatherings, email hello@coffey.cafe and we'll find a way to make it work.

Do you have vegetarian or gluten-free options? +

Always. We make room for different appetites. Ask our team about today's menu and any allergens before ordering.

Can I order ahead for pickup? +

Yes. Add your favourites to the bag and choose pickup at checkout. Online payment in this preview is simulated; no charge is made.
